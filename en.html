FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Describe what you need — the AI writes the code. If something’s off, just tell it what to fix.

Is the AI included?

Yes: the built-in GeodeForge AI works right away, no keys or VPN (100 requests a month in Basic, 400 in Pro). You can also connect your own AI with no limits — there are free options (OpenRouter, Groq, a local model via Ollama). For the hardest mods Claude is the best.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Download the app and click “Try 3 days for free” — all Pro features, no key or payment. Once per computer. Later just enter a purchased key — your mods and settings stay.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

It’s a normal warning for apps without a paid digital signature. Click “More info” → “Run anyway”. The installer is downloaded only from this site (GitHub).

Have a promo code?

Enter it in the “Promo code” field when buying. Friends of GeodeForge users get 10% off with their referral code.

How do I get the key?

Right after payment the key is shown on screen and sent by email. In the app click “Activate”, enter your email, a password and the key.

Can I use it on two computers?

The account works on one device at a time. When you sign in on a new PC, you get an email or Telegram message with a “Confirm sign-in” button, and the old PC signs out.

What do I need?

Windows 10/11 and internet. Nothing to install for building mods — it runs in the cloud.

What if the key didn’t arrive?

Check Spam. If it’s not there — message us with the email you used for the purchase.
